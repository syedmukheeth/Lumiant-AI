# Scenario Dataset and Licence Audit

_22 Nov 2026. Builds on `AI_DOMAIN_REINFORCEMENT_RESEARCH.md` (19 Nov). Licences were read from each repository's licence metadata on GitHub (API `license` field) or from the dataset card. Nothing was downloaded into the repository, no agreement was accepted, and nothing was purchased._

**Rule applied:** public is not the same as permitted. A source is usable for the product only if its licence allows commercial use and redistribution of what we would ship. Model training on it also needs that permission. Anything unclear is excluded.

## What we generated instead

Because no openly licensed dataset covers manufacturing, BOM and supplier evaluation together, Lumiant generates its own deterministic synthetic data:

- **`demo-delta`** (`services/api/src/lumiant/synthetic/manufacturing.py`, `config/tenants/demo-delta.yaml`): fictional Demo Delta Industrial with 3 plants and DCs, 19 items, 6 BOMs (multi-level, phantom kit, revision change), approved and unapproved alternates, 7 suppliers (quality, price, lead time, expired certificate, unapproved, USD), delivery history with rejections and delays, open POs, 6 competing customer orders, a critical spare, duplicate bolts and a missing lead time. Every record is hand-authored, dated relative to the generation date and marked synthetic (`_dataset_info.synthetic = true`, tenant classification `synthetic`). All names are fictional.
- **Scenario fixtures** (`services/api/evals/scenarios/*.yaml`): 66 scenarios and 85 checks with known expected results. These are regression tests, not training data.

The planning engine is deterministic and needs no training. Nothing in this phase trains or fine-tunes a model.
